# Grade 5 content map

## Mathematics

Place value and number sense; whole-number operations; fractions and decimals; measurement and geometry; data, probability, and patterns.

## English

Reading literature and informational text; vocabulary in context; writing and organization; grammar and conventions.

## Science

Scientific investigation; matter and energy; sound and light; Earth, Moon and Sun; ecosystems and resources.

## History & Social Science

Virginia geography; Indigenous peoples and exploration; Colonial Virginia; Revolution and independence; Virginia government and civics.

Each chapter should eventually contain: vocabulary cards, a picture/model, a worked example, a drag/match activity, 10–20 original questions, a retry explanation, and a mastery check.
